Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 27, 2026

1. Client-Side Calculation & Zero Data Persistence

At SolarSavingsHQ, user privacy is paramount. Our solar sizing and financial payback calculators execute 100% client-side in your local browser. We do not store, log, or transmit your electricity bills, monthly kWh usage, or property specifications to remote servers.

2. Cookies & Google AdSense DART Cookies

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to SolarSavingsHQ and/or other sites on the Internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or by visiting www.aboutads.info.

3. CCPA / CPRA & GDPR Compliance

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), users have the right to request disclosure of collected data, request deletion of personal information, and opt out of any data sharing. If you make a request, we have 30 days to respond.

4. Contact Information

For privacy inquiries or data rights requests, contact our Data Protection Officer at: privacy@solarsavingshq.com.
