Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026. Fully compliant with Google AdSense Policies, GDPR, and CCPA/CPRA standards.

1. Overview & 100% Client-Side Computation

At SolarSavingsHQ, we prioritize user privacy above all else. Unlike conventional solar lead generation websites that harvest and sell power bill data, our core mathematical sizing algorithms execute 100% client-side in your web browser. We do not store, record, or transmit your electricity bills, kWh usage numbers, or address inputs to remote servers.

2. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Cookies

We use third-party advertising companies, including Google AdSense, to serve advertisements when you visit our website. These companies may use cookies and web beacons to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites on the Internet.

Google Advertising & DART Cookie Provisions:
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to SolarSavingsHQ and/or other sites across the web.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

3. Voluntary Quote Inquiries & Installer Matching

If you voluntarily choose to submit a solar quote request form or send an advisory inquiry, the contact information you provide (such as your name, email address, phone number, and ZIP code) is used exclusively to fulfill that specific service request or connect you with licensed solar installers upon your explicit instruction. We never sell your personal contact information to unverified third-party telemarketers.

4. GDPR (European Union) & CCPA/CPRA (California) Rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), visitors possess the right to:

  • Request disclosure of any personal data collected or processed.
  • Request the immediate deletion or erasure of personal information provided via contact forms.
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal data (SolarSavingsHQ does not sell user data).
  • Non-discrimination for exercising your legal privacy rights.

5. Contact Our Data Protection Officer

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your CCPA/GDPR data rights, please contact our privacy compliance desk at: privacy@solarsavingshq.com.
