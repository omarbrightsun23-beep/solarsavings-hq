Residential Solar Engineering & Financial FAQ

Expert technical answers covering federal tax credits (IRC §25D), battery arbitrage, degradation factors, roof lifespans, and financing economics.

Federal Tax Credit

How does the 30% Federal Solar Tax Credit work under Section 25D?

Under Section 25D of the U.S. Internal Revenue Code (Residential Clean Energy Credit), homeowners claim a direct 30% dollar-for-dollar deduction against federal income taxes. It covers solar panels, microinverters, string inverters, racking, electrical wiring, labor, and battery storage (3kWh+). Any unused tax credit rolls forward into subsequent tax years.

IRC §25D • Direct Tax Offset
California NEM 3.0

Why is battery storage required for optimal ROI under California NEM 3.0?

NEM 3.0 (Solar Billing Plan) reduced daytime grid export compensation by ~75% down to avoided-cost rates (~$0.05–$0.08/kWh). Coupling an energy storage system (e.g. Tesla Powerwall 3 or EcoFlow) allows self-consumption during peak evening utility tariff windows ($0.50–$0.65/kWh from 4 PM to 9 PM).

Peak Shaving & Outage Backup
Engineering Physics

What is the exact mathematical formula to size a solar array?

Array size in kW = Monthly kWh ÷ (Daily Peak Sun Hours × 30 days × 0.80 Derate). The 0.80 derate factor (or 80% Performance Ratio) accounts for DC-to-AC inverter conversion efficiency, thermal derating in hot climates, wiring resistance, and ambient soiling loss.

NREL PVWatts v8 Compliant
Equipment Lifespan

What is the expected lifespan and degradation rate of Tier-1 solar panels?

Modern Tier-1 N-Type TOPCon and Heterojunction (HJT) monocrystalline solar modules carry 25 to 30-year linear power warranties. Degradation is typically ~1.0% in Year 1 and 0.35% to 0.50% annually thereafter, guaranteeing 85%–90% of nameplate production capacity at Year 25.

25–30 Year Linear Warranty
Roof & Structural

Do I need to replace my roof before installing residential solar panels?

If your asphalt shingle roof has less than 8 to 10 years of remaining lifespan, replacing it prior to or concurrently with solar installation is strongly advised. Detaching and reinstalling an existing array for a later roof replacement typically costs $2,500 to $5,000 in labor.

Structural Assessment Guide
Financing Economics

Should I purchase solar with cash, take a solar loan, or enter a lease/PPA?

Direct cash purchase and low-interest clean energy loans offer the highest lifetime return on investment (ROI) because the homeowner retains the 30% Federal Tax Credit and all local SREC credits. Leases and PPAs offer $0 upfront cost, but the third-party developer retains the tax credits.

Ownership vs. Third-Party PPA
Inverter Architectures

What is the difference between Microinverters and String Inverters with DC Optimizers?

Microinverters (e.g. Enphase IQ8) perform DC-to-AC conversion at each individual module, eliminating single points of failure and maximizing shade mitigation. String inverters with DC optimizers (e.g. SolarEdge) optimize at the module level while utilizing a centralized inverter, often reducing upfront hardware costs.

Enphase IQ8 vs. SolarEdge vs. String
Property Valuation

Does installing solar panels increase my home's property value or property taxes?

According to National Renewable Energy Laboratory (NREL) and Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory studies, owned solar panels increase home equity by an average of $4.00 per installed watt (~$20,000 on a 5kW system). Furthermore, over 38 states provide statutory property tax exemptions for residential solar additions.

NREL & LBNL Equity Studies
Grid Interconnection

What is Permission to Operate (PTO) and how long does the utility process take?

Permission to Operate (PTO) is the formal approval granted by your local utility allowing your solar array and bi-directional meter to energize and export power to the grid. PTO follows municipal building and electrical inspections, typically taking 2 to 6 weeks depending on utility backlog and grid interconnection requirements.
