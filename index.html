Federal Tax Credit

How does the 30% Federal Solar Tax Credit work under Section 25D?

Under Section 25D of the U.S. Internal Revenue Code, homeowners claim a direct 30% deduction against federal income taxes covering panels, inverters, racking, labor, and battery storage (3kWh+).

IRC §25D • Direct Tax Offset
California NEM 3.0

Why is battery storage required for optimal ROI under California NEM 3.0?

NEM 3.0 reduced daytime grid export compensation down to avoided-cost rates ($0.05–$0.08/kWh). Coupling energy storage allows self-consumption during peak evening utility tariff windows ($0.50–$0.65/kWh).

Engineering Physics

What is the exact mathematical formula to size a solar array?

Array size in kW = Monthly kWh ÷ (Daily Peak Sun Hours × 30 days × 0.80 Derate), where 0.80 accounts for inverter conversion, thermal derating, and ambient soiling loss.
