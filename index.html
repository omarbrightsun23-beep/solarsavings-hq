Residential Solar Engineering & Financial FAQ

Federal Tax Credit

How does the 30% Federal Solar Tax Credit work under Section 25D?

Under Section 25D of the Internal Revenue Code, homeowners claim a direct 30% dollar-for-dollar deduction against federal income taxes covering panels, inverters, racking, labor, and battery storage (3kWh+).

California NEM 3.0

Why is battery storage required for optimal ROI under California NEM 3.0?

NEM 3.0 slashed daytime export compensation by ~75%. Adding a battery lets homeowners bank midday solar to avoid peak evening utility rates ($0.50+/kWh between 4 PM and 9 PM).

Engineering Physics

What is the exact mathematical formula to size a solar array?

Array size in kW = Monthly kWh ÷ (Daily Sun Hours × 30 × 0.80 Derate). The 0.80 derate factor captures inverter conversion, temperature, and wire loss.
