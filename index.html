Residential Solar Engineering & Financial FAQ

Engineered answers to crucial homeowner questions covering Section 25D tax credits, utility net billing tariffs (NEM 3.0), system sizing mathematics, battery storage arbitrage, and long-term asset warranties.

Federal Tax Credit (ITC) IRC §25D

How does the 30% Federal Solar Tax Credit work, and what if I lack tax liability?

Under Section 25D of the U.S. Internal Revenue Code (Residential Clean Energy Credit), homeowners claim a direct 30% dollar-for-dollar reduction against their federal income tax liability. It covers panels, inverters, racking, labor, permitting, and standalone battery storage (3kWh+). If your tax credit exceeds your federal tax owed in Year 1, the remaining balance rolls over indefinitely to future tax years via IRS Form 5695.

California NEM 3.0 CPUC Net Billing

Why is battery storage crucial under California NEM 3.0 to achieve fast payback?

Under CPUC NEM 3.0 (PG&E, SCE, SDG&E), daytime solar grid export compensation was slashed by ~75% to ~$0.05–$0.08/kWh (Avoided Cost Calculator), while evening grid power costs $0.45–$0.65/kWh between 4 PM and 9 PM. Adding home lithium storage lets you bank midday surplus power for evening self-consumption, capturing peak arbitrage and slashing payback periods from 11+ years down to 5.2–6.8 years.

Engineering Physics NREL PVWatts v8

What is the exact mathematical formula to size a solar array from monthly kWh?

Array size in DC Kilowatts is calculated using NREL PVWatts engineering parameters:
kW = Monthly kWh ÷ (Daily Peak Sun Hrs × 30 Days × 0.80 Derate) The 0.80 derate factor accounts for DC-to-AC inverter conversion losses (~4%), module thermal coefficients at 25°C+ (~8%), wire resistance (~2%), and light soiling (~6%).

Financing Models Cash vs Loan vs PPA

Cash Purchase vs. Solar Loan vs. Lease/PPA: Which yields the highest 25-yr ROI?

Direct Cash Purchase delivers the highest lifetime ROI (breakeven in 5–8 yrs; zero interest overhead). Solar Loans provide instant bill savings with zero cash down, though dealer origination fees (15%–30%) can lengthen payback. Third-party Leases/PPAs forfeit the 30% tax credit to the leasing corporation and often include 2.9% annual cost escalators with UCC-1 liens that complicate home resale.

Grid Resilience UL 1741 Safety

Will my solar panels keep powering my home during a blackout without a battery?

No. Standard grid-tied inverters automatically shut down within 100 milliseconds during a utility outage under mandatory IEEE 1547 / UL 1741 anti-islanding regulations to prevent dangerous electrical feedback that could electrocute utility line workers. To maintain uninterruptible power during blackouts, you must pair panels with an islanding-capable hybrid inverter and a battery storage system (e.g. EcoFlow, Enphase 5P, Tesla Powerwall).

Roofing & Structural Detach & Reset

What happens if my roof needs replacement in 5–10 years? Should I reroof first?

If your asphalt shingle roof is over 15 years old, replace it prior to solar installation. Removing and reinstalling a solar array for a future roof replacement (Detach & Reset) costs $2,500 to $5,000. When reroofing concurrently with solar, the structural roof decking required for mounting solar may sometimes qualify for combined financing options (consult a certified CPA regarding Section 25D boundaries).

Hardware Lifespan Tier-1 Equipment

How much do solar panels degrade, and what does a 25-year warranty guarantee?

Modern Tier-1 N-type TOPCon and monocrystalline PERC solar panels experience a linear degradation rate of only 0.35% to 0.50% per year. Premium linear output warranties legally guarantee that your panels will still generate 85% to 92% of their original rated wattage at Year 25. Microinverters (like Enphase IQ8) carry 25-year warranties, while central string inverters typically require one replacement around Year 12–15 ($1,500–$2,200).

Home Equity LBNL Valuation

Will solar increase my property taxes and my home resale appraisal value?

According to research by the Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory (LBNL) and Fannie Mae, owned solar panels increase home resale value by approximately $4.00 per installed Watt (an average equity boost of $18,000–$25,000). Crucially, 38+ U.S. states have statutory 100% property tax exemptions for residential renewable energy systems, ensuring your local assessed property tax does not increase.

Legal Rights Solar Access Acts

Can my Homeowners Association (HOA) legally block my solar installation?

In more than 28 states (including California, Texas, Florida, Arizona, Colorado, North Carolina, and New Jersey), state Solar Rights Acts legally prohibit HOAs from banning solar panels or imposing architectural rules that decrease system generation efficiency by more than 10% or increase installation cost by more than $1,000–$2,000. HOAs may only require reasonable aesthetic modifications that do not impair solar performance.

Clean Energy Income SREC-II & SuSI

What are SRECs and how can homeowners earn passive annual cash from solar?

In states with Renewable Portfolio Standards (RPS)—such as NJ (SuSI), MA (SMART), PA, MD, and Washington D.C.—homeowners earn one Solar Renewable Energy Certificate (SREC) for every 1,000 kWh (1 MWh) generated. Utilities purchase these certificates to comply with state clean energy quotas, generating $600 to $2,400+ per year in direct passive cash for homeowners on top of electric utility bill savings.
