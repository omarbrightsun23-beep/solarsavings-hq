Frequently Asked Questions

How does the 30% Federal Solar Tax Credit (IRC Section 25D) work?

Homeowners who purchase their system outright or via solar loan can deduct 30% of total installation costs directly from their federal tax liability. Unused portions roll over to future tax years.

What is NEM 3.0 and why should I add a battery?

Under CPUC Net Billing (NEM 3.0), daytime grid export credits decreased by roughly 75%. Storing daytime power in a battery allows you to avoid high peak-rate electricity charges in the evening.
