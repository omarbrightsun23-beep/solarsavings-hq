Frequently Asked Questions

How does the 30% Federal Solar Tax Credit (ITC) work under Section 25D?

Under Section 25D of the Internal Revenue Code, homeowners can claim a direct 30% credit against their federal tax liability for the total cost of solar equipment, battery storage (3kWh+), and labor with no maximum dollar cap.

Why is battery storage required for optimal ROI under California NEM 3.0?

California NEM 3.0 reduced export credits by ~75%. Storing daytime solar in a home lithium battery allows homeowners to avoid expensive evening peak rates ($0.50+/kWh) between 4 PM and 9 PM, shortening payback to 5.5 to 7.0 years.
