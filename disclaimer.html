Financial & Energy Disclaimer

Effective Date: August 2026

1. Non-Financial Advice Notice

SolarSavingsHQ is not a registered financial advisor, tax advisory firm, or licensed electrical contractor. The estimates provided on this site are approximations based on NREL PVWatts v8 mathematical models, average state utility tariffs from the U.S. EIA, and published statutory guidelines for Section 25D of the Internal Revenue Code.

2. Variable Site Conditions

Actual solar generation and net financial yields will vary based on physical site conditions including roof azimuth, pitch, tree shading, local building permit requirements, inverter clipping ratios, and utility rate structure revisions (such as CPUC NEM 3.0 / Net Billing Tariff).

3. Tax Consultation

Consult a certified CPA or tax professional to determine your personal tax liability and eligibility for the 30% Federal Clean Energy Tax Credit.
