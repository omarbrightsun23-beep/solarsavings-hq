Financial & Engineering Disclaimer

Important disclosures regarding tax calculation modeling, engineering estimations, and installer quotes.

1. Not Financial, Tax, or Legal Advice

The calculations, estimations, and projections generated on SolarSavingsHQ are provided for informational and educational purposes only. They do not constitute formal financial, certified accounting (CPA), tax, or licensed engineering advice. You should consult with a licensed tax professional to confirm your eligibility for federal (IRC Section 25D) and state clean energy tax credits.

2. Mathematical Modeling Basis

Calculations utilize standard industry reference parameters derived from the National Renewable Energy Laboratory (NREL) PVWatts v8 model, assuming standard 0.80 system derate factors, 3% compounding annual utility inflation, and 0.50% annual panel degradation. Actual solar generation depends on specific roof tilt, shading, weather patterns, azimuth orientation, and equipment selection.

3. FTC Affiliate & Referral Disclosure

SolarSavingsHQ may receive referral commissions or compensation from vetted clean energy partners, battery manufacturers, or installer networks when users request custom quotes or purchase products through affiliate links. This compensation does not affect the objectivity or mathematical accuracy of our open-source calculators.
